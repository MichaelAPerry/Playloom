# Code of Conduct

Playloom is a family project in every sense: it is made for children, parents and teachers, and many of
them will also be contributors. Everyone taking part in the project's spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions and any future community channels — is expected to follow this code.

## Our standards

Please:

- be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and young contributors;
- assume good intent and give feedback about the work, not the person;
- accept responsibility and apologise when you get something wrong.

Do not:

- harass, insult or demean anyone, or make sexualised comments or advances of any kind;
- publish anyone's private information;
- post content that is inappropriate for a project used by children.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code and may temporarily or permanently
exclude people who do. To report a problem, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub
profile; reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.

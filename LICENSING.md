software under the **GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version**
(see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). If you distribute a modified Playloom, you must make your changes available
under the same license.

## Games and assets you create

A Playloom game package is data — scenes, objects, rules and assets — that the Playloom runtime reads.
The project's position is that such a package is an independent work and **not** a derivative of
Playloom:

> Games, game packages, templates you have modified, and assets created with or for Playloom belong to
> their authors, who may distribute them under any terms they choose, free or commercial. Running a
> game in Playloom, or exporting it from Playloom Studio, places no licensing obligation on the game.

Shared games should state their license in the package manifest. For assets you did not make yourself,
you are responsible for having the right to share them; Studio will ask for a license and credit when
assets are imported.

## SDK: game format types and validator

So that there is never doubt for tool and extension authors, `packages/sdk` — the game-format type
definitions and validator — is published under the **MIT License** (`packages/sdk/LICENSE`). Starter
templates will be too.
